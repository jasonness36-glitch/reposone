# Jeriah Broker LLC — 10-Minute Demo Script

Printable script for live demos. Lines marked **SAY** are word-for-word — read them as-is. Everything else is a stage direction for you.

## Before you start
- [ ] Demo sub-account open, pipeline visible on screen
- [ ] Your phone in hand, tracking number ready to dial
- [ ] Doc's Handyman case study open in another tab
- [ ] Ringer/notifications on so the text-back is visible when it lands

---

## 1. The Hook (30 seconds)

**SAY:**
"Quick question before I show you anything — when you're on a job and you miss a call, what happens to that customer?"

*(Let them answer. Most owners say something like "I call them back later" or "they probably call the next guy.")*

**SAY:**
"That's exactly the problem I'm going to show you how to fix in the next ten minutes."

---

## 2. The Live Wow Moment (2 minutes)

*(Pull out your phone in front of them. Dial the demo tracking number.)*

**SAY:**
"I'm calling my demo number right now, and I'm going to hang up without leaving a message — just like a real customer would if you didn't pick up."

*(Let it ring once or twice, then hang up. Turn your screen so they can see it.)*

**SAY:**
"Watch this..."

*(Within seconds, the missed-call text arrives: "Hey, sorry we missed you! This is Doc's Handyman — what can we help with?")*

**SAY:**
"That text just went out automatically, in under ten seconds, without anyone touching a phone. That's a customer who would've called your competitor next — instead, they're already texting you back."

---

## 3. The Machine Tour (3 minutes)

Show only these three things. Don't wander into anything else.

**A. The Pipeline**
**SAY:**
"This is where every lead lands the second they call, text, or fill out a form. You can see who's new, who's been quoted, who's scheduled, and who's paid — all at a glance."

*(Point to 2-3 cards in different stages.)*

**B. Automated Google Review Requests**
**SAY:**
"Every time a job gets marked complete, this automatically texts the customer asking for a Google review. You don't have to remember to ask — it just happens."

**C. Self-Booking Calendar**
**SAY:**
"And if a customer wants to book themselves, they don't have to call and play phone tag with you. They pick a time that works, right here, and it lands straight on your calendar."

---

## 4. Proof (1.5 minutes)

**SAY:**
"I'm not just telling you this works — here's a local example."

*(Show the Doc's Handyman case study.)*

**SAY:**
"[Insert the one before/after sentence from the case study here.]"

---

## 5. The Close (1 minute) — then stop talking

**SAY:**
"Setup is $___, and then it's $___ a month after that. I can have this live and working for you by Friday. Want me to start?"

*(Stop talking. Let the silence sit. Whoever speaks next loses the negotiation — let them go first.)*

---

## Objections & Responses

**"It's too expensive."**
**SAY:**
"I get it — let's look at it differently. How much is one missed call worth to you if it turns into a job? *(Wait for an answer.)* This pays for itself the first time it saves you one call you would've lost. Everything after that is profit."

**"I'll think about it."**
**SAY:**
"Totally fair. Can I ask what specifically you want to think through — the price, the timing, or whether it'll work for your business? *(Listen, then answer that one thing directly.)*"

**"I already have a website."**
**SAY:**
"That's great — a website's part of this too. But a website doesn't call a customer back when you miss them, doesn't ask for reviews automatically, and doesn't let people book themselves. This works alongside what you already have — it just makes sure no lead falls through the cracks."

---

*Keep the whole thing to 10 minutes. If they want to go deeper on one piece, that's a good sign — let them, then circle back to the close.*
